Why We Built This

Millions of people in India struggle to rebuild their lives because they lack a safe place to stay. An Urgent Home creates a trusted bridge between individuals in need and households willing to help. By combining technology, NGO partnerships, and verification systems, we aim to create a scalable and sustainable social impact solution.
